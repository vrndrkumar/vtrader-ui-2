Price Trigger Levels — The Core Feature

This is what makes VTrader unique. Place a horizontal price level on any chart. When the live price touches or crosses that level, VTrader automatically opens the Order Window so you can confirm and fire the trade instantly — no watching required.

How to place a trigger

  • Use the Drawing toolbar to draw a horizontal line
  • Set it as an active trigger level
  • When price hits it, Order Window fires
  • Confirm direction, lots, and price type
  • Order placed across all selected brokers

Real-world use case

  • NIFTY approaching a key resistance at 23,200
  • Set trigger at 23,200 for a breakout buy
  • Walk away — VTrader watches it for you
  • When NIFTY hits 23,200, order window appears
  • Confirm in 2 seconds — trade placed instantly

Index Bracket Orders

Place a symmetric bracket order around current price directly from the chart. VTrader calculates both the entry and stop, draws them on the chart, and waits for price to reach either side — then fires the corresponding order.

ℹ️
InfoBracket orders are ideal for range strategies — define your upper and lower bounds, and the platform handles the rest based on which level is touched first.
Module 03

Order Execution

A clean, focused order window that keeps the friction minimal and the information clear. Built for speed without sacrificing control.

The Order Window

A modal that opens automatically when a price trigger fires, or manually from the chart. Green header for BUY, red for SELL. Every relevant detail is pre-filled — you just confirm.

Order parameters

  • Market — executes at best available price
  • Limit — executes only at your specified price
  • SL-Limit — stop-loss with a limit price
  • Product: Normal (carry) or MIS (intraday)
  • Lot size auto-calculated per index per broker

Multi-broker execution

  • One Order Window — all connected brokers shown
  • Set different lot quantities per broker
  • All orders fire simultaneously
  • Per-broker status: Queued → Sent → Filled / Failed
  • Total lots and quantity shown before confirming
⚠️
ImportantFor SL-Limit orders, both a trigger price and a limit price are required. The order fires only when the trigger is touched, and executes at or better than the limit.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions traders ask when getting started.

Does VTrader place orders automatically without my confirmation?
When a price trigger is hit, the Order Window opens and waits for your confirmation. You still choose the order type, lots, and click Place Order. VTrader never places an order fully automatically without your input — it just brings the window forward at exactly the right moment so you don't miss the opportunity.
Can I connect more than one broker account?
Yes. VTrader supports multiple broker accounts simultaneously. In the Order Window, you can set different lot quantities for each broker and place orders across all of them with one click. This is ideal for traders who want to split execution across accounts.
How does the P&L on the Dashboard update?
Today's P&L combines realized P&L (from closed trades, fetched from the backend) and unrealized P&L (calculated live using WebSocket tick data). Every time a tick arrives for an open position's symbol, the unrealized P&L is recalculated instantly — no manual refresh needed.
What's the difference between the Journal and Reports?
The Journal is for reviewing individual trades — you can filter, sort, annotate, and mark trades as reviewed. Reports is for aggregate performance analysis — cumulative P&L curves, win/loss ratios, strategy comparisons, P&L distribution, and a calendar view. Use Journal for micro-analysis and Reports for macro-analysis.
How often does the AI Stock Insights engine run?
The engine analyses all stocks in the universe daily. The results you see on the Stock Insights page reflect the latest completed analysis run. Admins can trigger manual re-runs, and the batch progress bar appears when an analysis is in progress.
Can I trade equity (stocks) directly, or only options and futures?
VTrader's Trade screen is currently optimized for index options and futures (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, SENSEX). The Holdings module tracks your equity positions derived from trade history. Equity trades placed through the broker are tracked in Holdings and Journal automatically.
Is VTrader available on mobile?
VTrader is a web application and is responsive — it adapts to smaller screens. The Dashboard, Journal, Reports, and Insights sections work well on mobile. The Trade screen with multiple charts is best experienced on a desktop or laptop for the full workflow.
What do the Discovery, Transition, and Momentum scores mean?
Discovery Score measures how early you are in the opportunity — high Discovery means the stock is just starting to show strength before the crowd notices. Transition Score measures how actively the stock is breaking out. Momentum Score measures established, sustained uptrend strength. A high Discovery + low Momentum = early-stage gem. High Momentum = confirmed trend, potentially later entry but higher conviction.
