01 System Overview #

Stock Insight is a market research intelligence platform. Every analysis run scans the entire stock universe and answers one question per stock: "does this stock deserve investigation, because it may be early in a big move?"

What Stock Insight is NOT It is not a tip service and not a trading system. It produces no buy/sell/hold signals, no entry prices, no stop losses, no targets. It ranks stocks by research interest and explains its evidence. Every decision — and all homework on the company's business, results and news — belongs to the user.

Design principles

Evidence, not opinion

Every score comes from rules whose predictive value was measured on 2020–2026 history, walk-forward validated. Rules that failed testing were removed — even popular ones.

Explainability

Every point of every score maps to a visible, human-readable evidence line. No black boxes anywhere in the pipeline.

Early over obvious

Optimised to flag stocks before recognition, accepting a lower hit rate in exchange for earliness — that trade-off is the whole product.

Risk shown, never a veto

Many past big winners were small, volatile and illiquid. A "safety filter" would delete exactly the stocks the product exists to find — so risk is displayed prominently but never silently removes a stock.

02 Analysis Methodology #

Data collected

DataSourceDepthUsed for
Daily OHLCV candlesCandle APIfrom 2022 (~900 days)All signal computation
NIFTY daily candlesCandle APIsameRelative strength · market regime
Stock metadataMySQL stock_mstr—Universe, filters, sector ranks
Weekly candlesderived — resampled from daily—Weekly trend & weekly turn

That is the complete input. Fundamentals, news, sentiment, options and intraday data are not used. Fundamentals/news are honestly reported as "not evaluated" rather than guessed; intraday was tested and removed (see §3).

Processing — the feature snapshot

Each stock's candles are reduced to a single feature snapshot: ~30 measurements describing its current condition. Every measurement uses only past data (no look-ahead). Long-horizon features degrade gracefully — a stock with 60 days of history gets short-term features computed and the rest marked unavailable, never faked.

Structure

Swing pivots, trend, higher lows, change of character

Momentum

RSI, ADX, moving-average posture

Volume behaviour

Dry-up ratio, OBV slope, volume ratio

Compression

Bollinger width percentile, base length, tightness

Location

52-week high/low distance, decline duration

Prior advance & RS

120-day change; strength vs NIFTY; weekly layer

Filtering — deliberately minimal

Only two filters exist: active stocks and ≥ 30 daily bars (the mathematical minimum for the short-term indicators). No market-cap floor, no liquidity floor — liquidity becomes a risk flag, not an exclusion, because aggressive filters delete hidden gems. Stocks below 30 bars are recorded as visible failures with reasons, never silently skipped.

From signals to the final output

STEP 1
Evidence checks

Boolean rules with fixed thresholds. Passing checks become evidence lines with points; failing checks join the "missing evidence" list.

STEP 2
Three scores + risk

Discovery, Transition, Momentum scored independently 0–100. Risk scored separately — never blended in.

STEP 3
Market context

Each score ranked vs every analysed stock (market / sector / industry) → percentiles; conviction stars computed.

STEP 4
Classification

Badge, lifecycle stage, warning tags, executive summary. Snapshot stored for history & trend tracking.

Conviction formula (1–5 ★):

// combined confidence — separate from the engine scores
conviction = 0.45 × bestEngineScore
           + 0.35 × marketPercentile
           + 0.20 × (100 − riskScore)

// stars: ≥78 → ★★★★★ · ≥64 → ★★★★ · ≥50 → ★★★ · ≥36 → ★★ · else ★

03 Timeframes #

Daily data for evidence · weekly for regime and confirmation · monthly context via long windows · intraday deliberately excluded.

TimeframeRoleWhy
DailyComputation core. All indicators, structure, volume, location.Deep reliable history; matches the product's weeks-to-months horizon.
Weekly derivedRegime lens. Weekly trend state and the weekly turn.The single strongest validated predictor in every test; the fresh turn is early information, the established state is confirmation.
Long windows120-day prior advance · 250-day location · 200-day averageMonthly-scale context without a separate monthly series.

Why no intraday (1m–4H)? An earlier engine version analysed 15m/30m/1H/4H with a "multi-timeframe alignment" score. Research removed it: intraday history isn't deep enough to validate over 2020–2026, and the daily+weekly version of alignment showed no measurable predictive value for this horizon. Stock Insight answers "is a multi-week/multi-month move being prepared?" — a question intraday data cannot improve.

The key validated interaction Compression only earns points after a prior advance. A daily squeeze in a stock that already demonstrated strength tested at 1.3× the base rate; the identical squeeze without prior strength tested below base rate and scores nothing.

05 Price Action Concepts #

Only concepts that survived validation are in production. Not used: candlestick patterns, classical chart patterns, liquidity sweeps, order blocks, fair-value gaps. CHOCH is the one SMC-adjacent concept that survived, in one specific context.

Market structure

Swing pivots (3-bar confirmation, no repainting) → trend (HH+HL / LH+LL / range), higher lows (+10 Transition), and change of character — first close above the last lower-high in a downtrend (+10, kept small: early but noisy).

Prior advance — the core concept

Has the stock already risen ≥30% in ~6 months? Strength that rests tends to continue (1.28×; every combination tested without it fell below base rate). Classic stage analysis, now with evidence.

Bases & compression

Base = price within ±8% of its 20-day average. Compression = Bollinger width in the tightest 30% with a base ≥10 bars. The coiled spring is real — only for springs already shown to be strong.

Volume (supply & demand)

Dry-up = supply exhausted. OBV rising quietly = accumulation. High volume + falling OBV = distribution warning. Volume expansion as a bullish signal was tested and removed — it flags moves already underway.

52-week location

≥15% below the high = "under-followed territory" (+10 — future big winners sat deeper below highs than peers, months early). Near the high with a weekly downtrend = validated trap → warning.

Support/resistance & regime

Pivot-cluster zones annotate the weekly chart only — zero score weight (no entries/exits exist to anchor). NIFTY vs its 200-day average is shown as risk context; deliberately not a scoring input (2020-23 artifact rejected).

06 Decision Logic #

Scores are simple additive sums, capped at 100 — no hidden weights, no netting between engines. There is no Buy/Sell/Hold: the "decision" is a classification.

The hidden-gem gap rule Discovery ≥50 and ≥20 points above Momentum → the report states: "the market has NOT yet recognised this stock." The reverse gap → "already recognised — later-stage opportunity."

Conflicts are never netted A stock can be Discovery 85 / Momentum 20 / Risk HIGH at once — all shown. Warning tags coexist with any badge. Risk never vetoes (no risk condition appeared ≥1.5× more often in failures than successes).

Immutable snapshots

Every analysis is a stored row (is_latest flags the current one) → instant universe reads, full score history, upgrade/downgrade detection.

Transparent failures

Unanalysable stocks are stored with per-symbol reasons and retried once for transient errors. A "Retry all failed" action re-runs only those.

Non-blocking batches

Full-universe analysis runs as a background job with live progress; single-stock refresh is synchronous (~1–2s).

08 Workflow — End to End #

1 · INPUT
Universe + candles

All active stocks queued; one candle request per stock + NIFTY.

2 · PROCESSING
Feature snapshot

Trailing-only measurements; graceful degradation under 260 bars; <30 bars → explained failure.

3 · ANALYSIS
Engines + risk

Evidence and missing-evidence lines with fixed points.

4 · VALIDATION
Market context

Ranks vs market/sector/industry; conviction stars.

5 · CLASSIFY
Badge + summary

Badge rules, lifecycle stage, warning tags, executive note.

6 · OUTPUT
Store & serve

Snapshot saved → dashboard, report, history, movers.

09 Worked Examples #

Example A — strong evidence, honestly classified

ABC-EQ, a small industrial stock: +47% in 120 days ✓ · Bollinger width 22nd percentile with a 34-bar base ✓ · volume 10d/50d = 0.62 ✓ · OBV rising ✓ · 24% below its 52-week high ✓ · weekly up (turned 7 weeks ago — not fresh) · above 200-SMA · ADX 17, RSI 54 · ATR 3.1% ⚠ · turnover ₹3.4cr ⚠.

EngineCalculationScore
DISCOVERY30 + 15 + 15 + 10 + 1080
TRANSITION12 + 15 + 1037
MOMENTUM30 (gate) + 20 + 15 + 10 + 1085
RISK20 + 10 (ATR) + 10 (liquidity)40 · MEDIUM

Highest engine = Momentum → badge MOMENTUM ESTABLISHED. The system refuses to over-sell: strong Discovery evidence alone doesn't make a hidden gem when the market clearly already participates. Conviction ≈ 4★ High.

The counterfactual that makes it a gem The same stock caught before its weekly trend established: Momentum gates to 0, Discovery 80 stands alone, the gap rule fires → HIDDEN GEM CANDIDATE with "early evidence is much stronger than current momentum." Catching that earlier window is what the platform is optimised for.

Example B — a recovery just starting

XYZ-EQ fell 55% over 18 months. This month: 200-SMA reclaimed (+30) · weekly turn (+25) · dry-up (+15) · higher low (+10) → Transition 80 → badge TRANSITION STARTED. Discovery stays modest (~25 — no prior advance; it was falling). The missing-evidence list tells the user exactly what hasn't happened yet, and Risk honestly shows the −55% drawdown factor.

Example C — nothing happening

PQR-EQ: no prior advance, wide bands, normal volume, weekly down, no higher low → all engines < 25 → QUIET + WEAK STRUCTURE. The report says so plainly and wastes nobody's time.

A Research Provenance #

The rule set is the survivor of a four-phase research programme on 2020–2026 NSE history (119-stock validated sample, ~25,000 observations, outcome labels from +10%/20 days to +100%/250 days).

Phase 1 · Baseline audit

The indicator-rich original scored AUC 0.502 on unseen data — a coin flip

A fitted model found the weekly trend dominant; volume expansion, CMF, BOS-points and the "overbought penalty" were flat or inverted → removed.

Phase 2 · Multi-horizon validation

Surviving evidence beat base rates at every horizon, 4/4 walk-forward folds

Ranking simulations showed the system is a screener, not a top-5 autopilot; big-winner capture 15–16% vs 10% random.

Phase 3 · Architecture discovery

One universal score cannot serve momentum and reversal simultaneously

Specialisation won in the reversal context (+10pp); coefficient sign-flips proved the point → the three-engine design. Risk separation validated.

Phase 4 · Early-discovery timing

54% of future +50% movers flagged at a median lead of ~7 months

Prior advance proven the core driver (combos without it fell below base rate); no failure-based veto cleared the evidence bar → no hard filters.

B Limitations & Disclaimer #

Known limitations — stated plainly

• Absolute hit rates are inflated by survivorship bias (delisted losers are absent from history). Comparisons between rules remain valid; absolute numbers flatter.

• The validated hit rate is ~32% of top-decile Discovery names reaching +50% within ~6 months — most candidates do not move. Ranking, not prediction.

• Sector relative strength is currently inactive (sector-index symbol mapping pending).

• Fundamentals and news are not evaluated — the platform says so instead of guessing.

• Weights change only through a re-run of the research protocol, never by hand.
